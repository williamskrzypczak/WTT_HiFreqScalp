👀 How to Read Signals

  1. Pivot Signals: 🟢 Green triangle up (Long) and 🔴 White triangle down (Short) mark key EMA crossovers.
  2. SMP Dots: 💛 Large yellow dots (S/W) signal strong momentum peaks; 💜 small purple dots indicate weaker setups.
  3. Table Analysis: The on-chart table shows RSI, ADX, volume, trend direction, and success probabilities. 📊
  4. Trend Duration: Table displays trend duration predictions and historical pattern analysis. ⏱️

🎨 Chart Visuals

  • Price Line Color: Changes based on bullish (teal), bearish (purple), or neutral (white) EMA relationships.
  • Table Rows: Display alert status, trading style, indicator values, SMP alerts, and trend stats. 📋
  • Orange Flashing: 🟠 Orange flashing indicates current trend exceeds historical average duration.

✅ Best Practices

  • Use success rate analysis but always apply your own risk management. 🛡️
  • Wait for confirmed signals (high volume, strong ADX, favorable success probability) before acting. ⏳
  • Be cautious during low signal confidence or high market noise periods. ⚠️
  • Review the on-chart table for comprehensive analysis before entering trades. 🔍
💡 Key Insight: The SMP success rate represents historical performance. High continuation rates suggest trend-following opportunities, while low rates may indicate reversal patterns with higher risk.
